Memorize. Recall. Arrive.

Hidden Steps

A calm memory game for iPhone: look into the open house, memorize the way through the maze – then the roof closes and you find it again from memory, step by step.

Coming soon on the App Store

How it works

  1. Memorize

    The roof is off. You have a few seconds to remember the way to the exit.

  2. Recall

    The roof closes. Steer your figure with the arrows – if you hit a wall, the screen flashes red.

  3. Arrive

    Find the exit and the roof lifts. Fewer mistakes, more stars.

Ways to play

Training Mode

Endless houses in four categories: Flat House, Perspective Shift, Floors and Sequence – with adjustable complexity and memorizing time.

Pip’s Training

Pip the bird watches how you play, finds your weak spots and builds houses that train exactly those.

Duel

Two phones, the same houses: scan the QR code and play against each other over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth – no internet needed.

Free to play

The Flat House in Training Mode is free, and you can join any duel. A one-time purchase unlocks the full version: all categories and settings, Pip’s Training, creating duels – and no more ads.
