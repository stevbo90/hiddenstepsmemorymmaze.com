Privacy Policy

for the game “Hidden Steps” (iPhone and Android) and this website · Last updated: November 2026

In short: You don’t need an account, and we never ask for your name or email address. Your progress stays on your phone. In the free game, Google AdMob shows ads. In the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland they are only personalized with your consent (details in section 4). With the full version there are no ads. The game sends anonymous game statistics to Unity only if you agree at the first start. Duels run directly between two phones, without a server.

2. Data on your device

The game stores your progress, stars, settings, your answers to the privacy questions, your profiles in Pip’s Training (the name you type and the color of your figure) and the history of the houses you played for the statistics on your phone. This data does not leave your device except through the services described below. If you delete the game, it is gone. Storing it is strictly necessary to run the game (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG).

3. Apple and Google services on your device

In-app purchase

You buy the full version through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google alone handle the purchase. We receive no payment data, only a confirmation that the full version was bought so that the game can unlock it. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR.

Pip’s tips

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Pip’s tips are phrased with Apple’s language model. This runs entirely on your iPhone; no data is sent to us or to Apple.

Sharing

If you tap Share on a house, your phone’s share sheet opens with the house code. You decide what to do with it; the game itself sends nothing.

Apple Distribution International Ltd., Hollyhill Industrial Estate, Hollyhill, Cork, Ireland, and Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, are responsible for their own services. More in Apple’s Privacy Policy and Google’s Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

In the free game, Hidden Steps shows ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google”): a short ad between two houses after every third house in Training Mode. If you have the full version, the ads never start and no data is sent to Google.

To load and show ads, Google processes technical data such as your IP address, approximate location (derived from the IP address), device type, operating system, app version, information about ads shown and tapped, and diagnostic and crash data.

Your choice depending on your region

  • EU/EEA, United Kingdom, Switzerland: At the first start, Google’s consent window asks whether Google and its advertising partners may use data for personalized ads and store or read it on your device. The legal basis is then your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR, § 25 (1) TDDDG). If you don’t consent, Google only shows non-personalized or limited ads; Google processes the data needed for this on the basis of legitimate interests (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR), for example for delivery, frequency capping and fraud prevention.
  • US states with their own privacy law (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas and Virginia): Google and its advertising partners may use data for personalized ads unless you opt out. You can opt out in the game under Settings › Ad privacy settings. You can still play everything; the ads are just less relevant. More in section 10.
  • All other countries: The game shows no consent window. Google may use data for personalized ads.
  • Advertising ID, in every country: Google receives your iPhone’s advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you agree in Apple’s prompt “Allow app to track your activity?”. On Android, Google uses the device’s advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in the Android settings under “Privacy › Ads”.

Changing your choice: You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future and object to the use – in the game under Settings › Ad privacy settings. This link appears where Google offers these options. You can turn tracking off in the iPhone settings under “Privacy & Security › Tracking”.

Google may also process data in the USA. Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More in Google’s Privacy Policy and in “How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services”.

5. Game statistics (Unity Analytics)

At the first start, the game asks whether it may count anonymously how the game is played. Only if you choose Yes, sure does it send game statistics to Unity Analytics, a service of Unity Technologies SF, 30 3rd Street, San Francisco, CA 94103, USA (“Unity”). The statistics show us which houses are too hard or too easy and where the game needs to get better. They are not used for advertising.

What is sent: a random identifier that is lost when you delete the app (no name, email, Apple ID or advertising ID), device model, operating system, app version, language and the country derived from your IP address; when and how long you play and whether you come back on later days; which houses you start and finish (game mode, category, complexity, stars); when the full version window appears, whether you buy it (with price) and when an ad was shown. With every connection, Unity processes technical data such as your IP address.

Changing your choice: You can turn it on or off at any time under Settings › Game statistics. If you turn it off, the game stops sending and asks Unity to delete the data collected so far for your device.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR, § 25 (1) TDDDG). Unity processes the data on our behalf (Art. 28 GDPR); transfers to the USA are based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. More in Unity’s Privacy Policy.

6. Duel

In a duel, two phones play directly with each other: in the same Wi-Fi or through a personal hotspot, or over Bluetooth. There is no server, and the data goes neither to us nor to third parties. Only the duel settings, which houses are played, whether you are ready, and your results (mistakes and time) are exchanged. For this, the QR code contains your phone’s addresses in the local network.

The game asks for these permissions: Camera only to scan the QR code (the images are neither stored nor sent), Local Network and Bluetooth for the connection. On Android, the Bluetooth permission is called “Nearby devices”; on older Android versions the system requires the location permission for it. The game only uses it to find the other phone and does not determine your location. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (a feature you use).

7. Children

The game is not specifically directed at children; the age rating is shown in the App Store or on Google Play. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that we have data about your child, write to support@hiddenstepsmemorymaze.com. We will then delete it.

8. This website

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking services and loads no content from other servers (the font is hosted here too).

It is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open the pages, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address to deliver the pages and protect them from attacks. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure, reachable website (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. More in GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

9. Your rights in the EU, the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7 (3)). Write to us at support@hiddenstepsmemorymaze.com. As we store no names or email addresses, it is hard for us to match data to you. The easiest way to delete the statistics data is to turn Game statistics off.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the authority where you live or the one responsible for us.

10. Your rights in the USA and other countries

If you live in a US state with its own privacy law, this section supplements the rest of this policy.

Which data is concerned and who receives it

  • Identifiers: IP address and the device’s advertising ID (on the iPhone only if you allow tracking), both for Google; IP address and the random identifier of the game statistics for Unity (only with your consent).
  • Approximate location, derived from the IP address.
  • Usage and device data: device type, operating system, app version, information about ads shown and tapped, and diagnostic and crash data (Google); device model, operating system, app version, playing times, houses played and purchases (Unity).

Google uses this data for advertising as described in section 4, Unity for the game statistics as described in section 5. Apple and Google are responsible for their own services (section 3).

“Sale” and “sharing”

We do not sell data to others for money. The free game does earn money with ads, however, and the use of data by Google and its advertising partners described in section 4 may count as a “sale”, “sharing” or “targeted advertising” under the law of some US states.

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information: To opt out, open Settings › Ad privacy settings in the game and choose there that your data should not be sold or shared.

Your rights

Depending on your state, you may have the right to know which data is processed and to get a copy, to have it corrected or deleted, to opt out of its sale, sharing and use for targeted advertising, and to appeal if we decline a request. If you use these rights, we will not treat you differently, and you can keep playing everything.

Write to us at support@hiddenstepsmemorymaze.com, including for an appeal. Google’s own processing for advertising is explained in Google’s Privacy Policy.

Other countries

If you live in another country, its law may give you similar rights. Just write to the same address.

11. Changes

If the game or the law changes, we will update this policy. The version published here applies.
