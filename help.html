Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

While the roof is open, memorize the way to the exit. Then the roof closes and you steer your figure with the arrows – it walks to the next turn or fork. If you hit a wall, the screen flashes red. In some houses the camera turns to the side on the way; the arrows then work the way you see the house. The full guide is in the game under How it works.

What does the full version include?

It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It unlocks all four categories, complexity and memorizing time, Pip’s Training and creating your own duels, and it removes the ads.

I bought the full version, but it isn’t active.

Open the game with an internet connection – the store then reports the purchase again. On a new device, tap Restore purchases in the full version window. If that doesn’t help, write to us with the date of the purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

When are ads shown?

Only in the free game: a short ad after every third house in Training Mode, when you tap to continue after the stars – never while you memorize and never in a duel. With the full version there are no ads. Where Google offers privacy options (for example in the EU and some US states), you can change your choice under Settings › Ad privacy settings. More in the Privacy Policy.

The duel doesn’t connect.

Both phones need to be in the same Wi-Fi, or one opens a personal hotspot for the other. Without Wi-Fi the game connects over Bluetooth – switch it on on both phones. On the iPhone, allow Local Network and Bluetooth for Hidden Steps (iPhone Settings › Hidden Steps). If the camera can’t read the QR code, type in the code shown below it. Creating a duel needs the full version; joining one is free.

Pip doesn’t talk.

Check Settings › Pip reads aloud and the volume – if your phone is muted, Pip stays silent. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Pip’s tips are phrased fresh each time; on other devices Pip uses ready-made sentences.

How do I change the language or switch to dark mode?

The game speaks English and German. You can switch the language and choose Light or Dark in the settings.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

Your progress is stored only on your phone; there is no account. If you set up the new iPhone from a backup or with Apple’s device transfer, it usually comes along. The full version can always be restored with Restore purchases.

How do I get my data deleted?

In the game under Settings › Reset progress – this deletes your progress on the phone. If you switch Game statistics off, the game also asks Unity to delete the statistics data of your device. Deleting the app removes everything else.
